\begin{proposition}[Gaussian closure computes the LMMSE estimator]
\label{prop:lmmse}
Let $\Pgauss f$ be the Gaussian density matching the mean and variance of $f/\!\int\!f$, and let
\emph{moment-projected sum-product} be the forward--backward recursion applying $\Pgauss$ to each
message immediately after every transition integral, multiplying by the site's unary likelihood
before the next. Take the chain \eqref{eq:prior} with $a_i = \corr a_{i-1} + \varepsilon_i$,
$\corr\neq0$, Gaussian unary likelihoods, $\E[a_1]=0$, an innovation law $\innov$ with mean zero
and finite variance $\ivar$, and an initial message Gaussian or projected by $\Pgauss$. Then it
returns the LMMSE estimator of the covariance-matched Gaussian model,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lmmse}
\lmmse
= \Cov(\va,\vx)\,\Cov(\vx)^{-1} \vx
= e^{-t}\Sig_0\left(e^{-2t}\Sig_0 + \Dt \Id\right)^{-1} \vx ,
\end{equation}
and returns the same estimator for every innovation law sharing the moments $(0,\ivar)$.
\end{proposition}
