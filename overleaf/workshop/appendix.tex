\section{Notation}
\label{app:notation}

$\nsites$ is the chain length, which is also the ambient dimension; $\nseq$ is the number of
observed sequences; $\ngrid$ is the number of quadrature points; $\kernel$ is always the Markov
transition kernel, $\innov$ the innovation density, $\corr$ the autoregressive coefficient and
$\ivar$ its variance. Vectors are bold lowercase, matrices bold uppercase. The forward process is
$\vx(t)=e^{-t}\va+\sqrt{\Dt}\,\vz$ with $\Dt=1-e^{-2t}$; the reverse SDE and its sign conventions
follow \citet{anderson1982,haussmann1986} and are stated in full in the companion paper.

\section{The score--posterior identity}
\label{app:identity}

$\Pt$ is a Gaussian convolution of $\Pz$, and
$\nabla_{\vx} g_{\Dt}(\vx-e^{-t}\va)=-\tfrac{\vx-e^{-t}\va}{\Dt}g_{\Dt}(\vx-e^{-t}\va)$, with the
integrand dominated uniformly whenever $\Pz$ has a finite first moment. Dominated convergence then
licenses differentiation under the integral; dividing by $\Pt(\vx)$ and recognising the posterior
density gives \eqref{eq:score}. This is Tweedie's formula
\citep{robbins1956,miyasawa1961,efron2011}. Since $\pmean{\va}$ minimises the conditional squared
risk, every error below is reported \emph{against} it rather than as raw risk: the Bayes floor is
common to all estimators, and subtracting it is what makes the comparison informative.

\section{Stationarity}
\label{app:stationary}

With $\ivar=1-\corr^2$ and $\Var(a_1)=1$, second moments propagate exactly: $\Var(a_i)=1$ and
$\Cov(a_i,a_j)=\corr^{|i-j|}$ for every innovation shape. The chain is therefore
\emph{covariance}-stationary, which is what makes the variance-matched family a controlled probe.
It is not strictly stationary for non-Gaussian $\innov$ --- the invariant law of
$a_i=\corr a_{i-1}+\varepsilon_i$ is that of $\sum_{k\ge0}\corr^k\varepsilon_{-k}$, which is not
Gaussian --- and we do not claim it is. The two notions coincide for Gaussians because a Gaussian
law is determined by its first two moments.

\section{Protocol}
\label{app:protocol}

One frozen configuration, defined in a single file and imported by every experiment so that no run
can diverge from it: Laplace chain unless stated, $\corr=0.85$, $\nsites=32$, $\ngrid=401$,
$\halfwidth=8$, twelve log-spaced noise levels on $[0.05,3.0]$, budgets
$\nseq\in\{32,\dots,4096\}$, and $256$ held-out sequences on a disjoint seed. The ring uses
$T=12$. EM stops when the fitted innovation excess kurtosis stops moving,
$|\Delta\kappa_4|<10^{-3}$, never when $\corr$ does: a settled $\corr$ certifies a
converged-looking trace over a kernel that is nowhere near converged.
