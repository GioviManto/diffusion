\small
\textbf{Who this is for.} A reader who knows undergraduate probability and linear algebra and
nothing about diffusion models, graphical models, or expectation maximisation. Every concept
used anywhere in the project is built here from its definition. Nothing is cited in place of
an explanation. Where a step is standard but non-obvious, it is done in full rather than
asserted, and where an assumption is doing real work, there is a box saying what breaks
without it.
